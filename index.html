Our Mission

AIDS is preventable and treatable. We work to make that a reality in every community — starting with knowledge, dignity, and access.

Educate

Know the Facts

Reliable information is the first line of defense.

  • Prevention: PrEP reduces the risk of getting HIV from sex by about 99% when taken as prescribed.
  • U=U: Undetectable = Untransmittable. People living with HIV who maintain an undetectable viral load cannot pass the virus to partners.
  • Testing: Regular testing is quick, often free, and the only way to know your status for sure.
  • Treatment: Modern treatment allows people with HIV to live long, healthy lives.
  • Stigma: Stigma keeps people from testing and care. Kindness saves lives.

Get Support

You're not alone. Help is available, confidential, and often free.

Find a Test

Local clinics, community health centers, and at-home test kits make testing easier than ever. Search for a free or low-cost testing site near you.

PrEP & Treatment

Talk to a healthcare provider about PrEP for prevention or treatment options if you're living with HIV. Many programs offer financial assistance.

Peer Support

Our peer-support network, led by Deshaun Moss, connects you with people who understand — for guidance, encouragement, and a listening ear.

Talk to Someone

If you need immediate support or have questions, reach out to a local HIV/AIDS service organization or a confidential helpline.
